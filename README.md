#  Adidas US Sales Performance & Strategic Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI analytics dashboard designed to monitor, evaluate, and optimize Adidas sales performance, operating profits, and distribution channels across the United States.

---

##  Project Overview
This project provides an in-depth analysis of Adidas's US sales metrics, operating profits, regional performance, and distribution methods. Built using **Power BI**, the dashboard equips decision-makers with actionable insights into revenue drivers, top-performing product lines, and geographic expansion opportunities.

---

##  Key Business Metrics
* **Total Sales Revenue:** $900M
* **Operating Profit:** $332.13M
* **Operating Margin:** 42.3%
* **Total Units Sold:** 2,000,000 Units (2M)
* **Average Unit Price:** $45.22

---

##  Key Analytical Insights

### 1. Regional & Profitability Breakdown
* **Top Profit Region:** The **West Region** leads with **$90M** in operating profit, followed by **Northeast ($68M)**, **South ($61M)**, **Southeast ($61M)**, and **Midwest ($53M)**.
* **Monthly Trends:** Sales show seasonal fluctuations throughout the year, peaking sharply during July and August.

### 2. Sales Methods & Product Performance
* **Units Sold by Sales Method:**
  * **Outlet:** 37.9%
  * **In-store:** 34.3%
  * **Online:** 27.8%
* **Top Product Category:** **Men's Street Footwear** dominates sales volume with **0.59M units sold**, followed by **Men's Athletic Footwear (0.44M)** and **Women's Apparel (0.43M)**.

### 3. Retailers & Geographic Reach
* **Key Retail Partners:** **West Gear** ($243M / $0.24bn) and **Foot Locker** ($220M / $0.22bn) generate the highest overall sales revenue.
* **Top States by Sales:** **New York** ($64.23M), **California** ($60.17M), and **Florida** ($59.28M) are the primary state-level drivers.

---

##  Tools & Technologies
* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* **Data Cleansing:** Power Query
* **Advanced Features:** What-IF Analysis, Decomposition Tree, and Interactive Navigation Buttons
